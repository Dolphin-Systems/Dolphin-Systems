FenceFleet: know where every panel is

Track panels, feet, and clamps across every job site, bill each hire on 28-day cycles with pro-rata math, and print invoices in one click.

2026-10-11

Temp fence companies lose three to five percent of their panels every year. Nobody steals a whole yard at once. Panels just drift: a few left at a job site after pickup, a couple damaged and never written down, a mid-hire extension nobody billed. For a 200-panel operator that is four to six thousand dollars a year, gone quietly.

What it does

FenceFleet tracks your whole fleet — panels, feet, and clamps — and shows what is out, what is available, and what was lost. Each job site gets its own hire record: deliveries, mid-hire additions, returns, and scheduled pickups. Billing runs on 28-day cycles with exact pro-rata math, so an extension that starts on a Tuesday is billed from that Tuesday, not from the next round number. When the hire closes, one click prints the invoice.

Who it's for

  • Temp fence rental companies tired of spreadsheet tracking
  • Event fencing outfits with short, overlapping hires
  • Contractors who rent fence to their own job sites
  • Anyone who has ever wondered where panel number 147 went

How to use it

  • Enter your fleet counts once: panels, feet, clamps, and what a replacement panel costs
  • Add a hire when panels leave the yard, with the site name and your rate
  • Record mid-hire additions and returns as they happen, including lost or damaged panels
  • Close the hire and print the invoice — or use the quick quote calculator before you quote a job

Your data stays in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere. FenceFleet is $15 a month, or $75 for six months. Pay the admin, request your unlock PIN, and the tool opens up for your whole subscription.
