MeetingCost: see what your meetings really cost

Add attendees with hourly rates, start the timer, and watch the real cost tick up live.

2026-09-30

Meetings feel free. They are not. MeetingCost puts the real number on screen while the meeting is happening.

What it does

Add each attendee with their hourly rate. Start the timer. A big ticker shows the running cost, updating every second, with a per-person breakdown underneath.

Why it helps

A visible cost changes behavior. Agendas get tighter. Invite lists get shorter. Meetings end on time.

  • Daily standups that drift
  • Client calls with five people on them
  • Workshops that run long
  • Any recurring meeting nobody questioned

How to use it

  • Add attendees and set hourly rates (presets included)
  • Enter minutes manually or start the live timer
  • Watch the ticker — pause or reset anytime

MeetingCost is $8 a month, or $40 for six months. Pay the admin, request your unlock PIN, and the tool opens up for your whole subscription.
