What it does

Built for a recurring job.

LoanScope calculates your real monthly payment, total interest, and payoff date for any loan. Add an optional extra monthly payment to see exactly how much interest it saves and how many months it shaves off. Year-by-year payoff table and principal-vs-interest breakdown included.

  • Monthly payment, total interest, and payoff date from amount, rate, and term
  • Extra-payment modeling: see interest saved and months cut
  • Year-by-year amortization table
  • Principal vs interest visual breakdown
  • Runs fully offline in your browser
