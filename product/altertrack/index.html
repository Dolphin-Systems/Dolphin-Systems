What it does

AlterTrack is a digital ticket board for alterations shops. Log each garment with customer, alterations, measurements, due date, and price. Move tickets from Received to Picked Up, find any ticket in seconds when a customer calls, and print claim tickets.

  • Ticket board: Received, In Progress, Ready, Picked Up
  • Tickets with customer, garment, alterations, measurements, and due dates
  • Price, deposit, and auto-computed balance on every ticket
  • Instant search by name, phone, garment, or ticket number
  • Due-today and overdue flags, plus printable claim tickets
