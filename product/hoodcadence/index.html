What it does

HoodCadence tracks every restaurant account against its NFPA-96 cleaning cadence — monthly for solid fuel, quarterly for high volume, semi-annual for moderate, annual for low volume. It flags overdue and due-soon sites, and prints a signed NFPA-96 service report after each visit.

  • NFPA-96 cadence engine: cooking type to cleaning frequency
  • Site register with automatic next-due dates
  • Overdue and due-within-14-days alerts
  • Printable NFPA-96 service reports with certificate numbers
  • One-click mark-cleaned that resets the schedule
  • All data saved in your browser
