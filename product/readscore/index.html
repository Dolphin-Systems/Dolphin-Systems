What it does

Built for a recurring job.

ReadScore analyzes any text and scores its readability. Paste in an email, article, or proposal and get word counts, reading time, Flesch Reading Ease and grade level, a sentence-length map that flags run-ons, and the words you lean on most. Everything runs in your browser — nothing you paste ever leaves the page.

  • Live word, character, sentence, and paragraph counts
  • Reading time and speaking time estimates
  • Flesch Reading Ease score and grade level
  • Sentence-length map that flags run-on sentences
  • Top-word frequency with stop words filtered out
