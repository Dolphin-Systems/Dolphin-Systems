What it does

CakeFlow is the order board for home bakers and custom cake shops. Every order moves from inquiry to quoted to booked to in production to ready, with pickup dates, servings, flavors, design notes, prices, and deposits tracked on each card. A pickup list view sorts it all by date so no order is ever a surprise.

  • Six-stage order pipeline: inquiry to done
  • Customer, pickup date/time, servings, flavor, and design notes per order
  • Deposits and balance-due math on every card
  • Pickup list view sorted by date
  • Stats: active orders, due this week, outstanding balances
  • Orders saved in your browser
