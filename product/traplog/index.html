What it does

Built for a recurring job.

TrapLog is the daily activity log for licensed nuisance wildlife control operators. Record every control activity with the fields state inspectors ask for — client, address, date, provider, species, number removed, control method, disposition — and keep it current at the business location. Filter by date, client, or species, print an inspection-ready daily log, and export a CSV when annual-report time comes. Everything stays in your browser.

  • Log every control activity with state-required fields
  • Species, method, and disposition picklists built for NWCO work
  • Search and filter by date, client, or species
  • Print an inspection-ready daily activity log
  • CSV export for annual activity reports
  • Data stays in your browser — nothing uploaded
