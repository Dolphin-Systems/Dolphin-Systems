What it does

SaltRoute keeps your water treatment routes on schedule. Register every customer's equipment, track salt refill cadences from your service history, and get a ready-to-run weekly delivery list — no printed tickets, no missed stops.

  • Customer equipment registry with model and serial number
  • Automatic salt refill due dates computed from service history
  • Weekly route list with overdue stops flagged
  • One-tap visit logging with salt bags, filter changes, and notes
  • Full service history per customer
  • CSV export for route planning and invoice prep
