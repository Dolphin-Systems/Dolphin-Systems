What it does

SelectSheet tracks every client selection on a remodel — tile, fixtures, paint, hardware — in one sheet organized by room, with allowances, vendor links, and due dates. You see what's pending, what's over budget, and the whole thing prints clean for the job file.

  • Selection sheets organized by room
  • Allowance vs. actual cost ledger per item and per project
  • Client approval tracking: pending, picking, approved
  • Milestone-tied due dates with overdue flags
  • Print-ready selection sheet / save as PDF
  • Save and reload projects in your browser
