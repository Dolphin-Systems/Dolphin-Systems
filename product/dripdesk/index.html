What it does

DripDesk is a formula builder and menu-pricing calculator for IV hydration clinics. Pick ingredients from the cost library (or add your own), assemble drip formulas from ready-made templates, dial in your margin, and print a clean menu card. Everything saves in your browser — no account, no backend, no spreadsheet.

  • Ingredient library with editable supplier costs
  • Seven ready-made drip formula templates
  • Live ingredient cost roll-up and margin dial
  • Suggested pricing from your target margin
  • Printable menu cards for the clinic counter
  • Formulas saved in your browser, exportable as JSON
