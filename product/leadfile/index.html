What it does

LeadFile keeps the EPA RRP record package for every renovation job on pre-1978 housing: Renovate Right pamphlet delivery, lead test results, the assigned certified renovator, crew training, and cleaning verification. Track firm and renovator certification expiries, and print a complete per-job compliance package to keep for the 3-year retention rule. Built for painters, remodelers, and trades who disturb paint in older homes.

  • Job register for pre-1978 work with test-or-assume-lead path
  • The full EPA RRP record package as a per-job checklist
  • Firm and renovator certification expiry countdowns
  • One-click printable compliance package per job
  • Everything stored in your browser with JSON backup
