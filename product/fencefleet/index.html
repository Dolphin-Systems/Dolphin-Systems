What it does

FenceFleet tracks your temp fence fleet — panels, feet, and clamps — across every job site, and bills each hire on 28-day cycles with exact pro-rata math. Record deliveries, returns, and losses; print hire invoices in one click. Built for temp fence rental companies.

  • Fleet dashboard: panels, feet, and clamps owned, out, and lost
  • Per-site hire records with deliveries, returns, and pickup scheduling
  • 28-day hire billing with exact pro-rata extensions
  • Lost and damaged panel logging with replacement cost
  • One-click printable hire invoices
  • All data stays in your browser — works offline
