What it does

RenewGuard tracks every contract, subscription, domain, license, and policy by the date that actually matters: the notice deadline. Enter the renewal date and how much notice the contract requires, and it works out your last day to cancel or renegotiate, sorts everything by urgency, and shows the yearly spend on the line. Built for small businesses tired of paying for another term they meant to end.

  • Notice deadline calculated automatically from renewal date and notice days
  • Contracts sorted by urgency - act now, decide soon, window missed, safe
  • Yearly spend tracked, plus money sitting inside a closing notice window
  • Mark renewed (date rolls forward) or cancelled in one click
  • Print a renewal report or export everything as CSV
  • Saved in your browser - no account, no uploads
