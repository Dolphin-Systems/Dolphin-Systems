What it does

CashRoute gives independent ATM route operators one place to track machines, audit processor statements for fee errors, forecast cash refills, and log uptime — with a monthly per-machine route report that used to live in spreadsheets.

  • Machine and location registry with cash-on-hand tracking
  • Processor statement CSV import with automatic fee-anomaly flags
  • Cash load log with days-remaining refill forecasts
  • Outage log with trailing 30-day uptime per machine
  • Monthly per-machine route report, printable
